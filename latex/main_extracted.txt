                                                             Abstract

Accurate detection of pavement distresses such as cracks and potholes is critical for infrastructure
safety maintenance. However, existing YOLO-based methods still face challenges including insuf-
ficient feature representation, computational redundancy, and lack of morphological consistency
supervision in the loss function, especially when dealing with the extreme morphological differences
between elongated cracks and compact potholes. To address these issues, this paper proposes BMS-
YOLO (Box-supervised Morphology-aware Sparse YOLO), a lightweight pavement distress detection
model under pure box supervision. At the feature extraction level, a Frequency-Direction Detail
Enhancement (FDDE) module is designed to decouple the feature map into high- and low-frequency
branches and strengthen crack edge responses via directional convolutions. Within the C2f blocks,
a Morphology-aware Sparse Mixture of Experts (MorphSparseMoE) is embedded to dynamically
activate four types of experts (horizontal-line, vertical-line, isotropic, and regional) via a routing
mechanism, enabling morphology-adaptive modeling with low computational cost. Meanwhile, Effi-
cient Channel Attention (ECA) and a lightweight SPPF-CSPC structure are introduced to further
enlarge the receptive field and reduce parameters. At the loss function level, we replace the con-
ventional CIoU with Wise-IoU (WIoU) which introduces a center-distance-based dynamic focusing
weight, and add a novel box morphology consistency loss that constrains aspect ratio and area in log
space, significantly improving shape supervision for elongated cracks. Furthermore, a post-processing
pipeline with confidence calibration and topology-guided Union-Find-based box fusion is designed to
effectively mitigate fragmented repeated predictions in crack detection. Experiments on a self-built
pavement distress dataset and public CFD and DeepCrack datasets show that BMS-YOLO achieves
89.7% mAP and 221 FPS inference speed, outperforming state-of-the-art segmentation and detection
models in both accuracy and efficiency, validating the effectiveness of each improved module and the
strong generalization capability of the model. This work provides an efficient and robust solution for
real-time pavement distress detection under pure box supervision.
